Q-01

Are your roof inspections really free?

+

Yes, every inspection is free with no obligation. We check your roof's real condition, show you what we find, and give you an honest answer. If your roof is fine, we'll tell you that too.

Q-02

Will my homeowner's insurance pay for my roof?

+

If the damage came from a covered event like wind or hail, your policy usually pays for repairs or replacement minus your deductible. We document the damage, help you file, and meet your adjuster on-site so nothing gets missed.

Q-03

How long does a roof replacement take?

+

Most residential roof replacements take one to two days once materials arrive, depending on the size and pitch of your roof and the weather. You get an exact timeline in your written quote before we start.

Q-04

Should I repair my roof or replace it?

+

It depends on the age of your shingles, how widespread the damage is, and the condition of the decking underneath. If a repair will genuinely hold, we'll quote the repair. We don't push replacements that aren't needed.

Q-05

What shingles do you install?

+

Owens Corning Duration® with SureNail® Technology and Atlas Pinnacle® Pristine with Scotchgard™ Protector. Both are architectural systems rated for 130 MPH wind and backed by limited lifetime warranties.

Q-06

What areas do you serve?

+

Alexandria, Pineville, Ball, Tioga, Boyce, Woodworth and the surrounding Central Louisiana communities.
