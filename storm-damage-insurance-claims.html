Q-01

Will my homeowner's insurance pay for my roof?

+

If the damage came from a covered event like wind or hail, your policy usually pays for repairs or replacement minus your deductible. We document the damage, help you file, and meet your adjuster on-site so nothing gets missed.

Q-02

Are your roof inspections really free?

+

Yes, every inspection is free with no obligation. We check your roof's real condition, show you what we find, and give you an honest answer. If your roof is fine, we'll tell you that too.
