What it actually is

Liquid rubber.
One seamless skin.

Elastic rubber coatings — you'll also see them called elastomeric or rubberized coatings — are thick liquid-applied waterproofing membranes. They go on wet and cure into a single continuous rubber layer with no seams, laps or fasteners to leak.

  • It stretches instead of cracking. High-elongation formulations stretch several times their own length, so the membrane moves with your roof through 100° summers and February cold snaps.
  • It bridges the small stuff. A full-thickness film spans pinholes, seams, fastener heads and hairline cracks and locks them under one sealed surface.
  • It fights moisture, rust and UV. A seamless barrier keeps water off metal and masonry and takes the sun's punishment instead of your roof.
  • It reflects heat. A bright reflective finish sends solar energy back up instead of pushing it into the building — a real difference in a CenLA August.
  • No tear-off. We coat over the existing surface, so there's no demolition, no dumpster in the driveway and far less disruption.
  • It's renewable. When the film eventually wears, the roof gets recoated — not replaced.

Straight answers

Coating
questions answered.

Q-01

What is an elastic rubber coating?

+

An elastic rubber coating — also called an elastomeric or rubberized coating — is a thick liquid-applied waterproofing membrane. It goes on by roller, brush or sprayer and cures into one seamless rubber skin over the whole surface. Because it stays flexible, it expands and contracts with the roof instead of cracking, which is what keeps the seal watertight through Louisiana heat and cold snaps.

Q-02

What kind of roof can be coated?

+

Flat and low-slope roofs, metal roofs and metal buildings, mobile and manufactured homes, RV and trailer roofs, modified bitumen and rolled roofing, plus concrete, masonry, patios, gutters and flashing details. It is not a substitute for shingles on a steep-slope shingle roof — if that's what you have, we'll tell you straight and quote the right fix instead.

Q-03

How long does an elastic rubber roof coating last?

+

It depends on the product, the film thickness and the condition of the roof underneath. A properly prepped roof coated at full spec — typically a 10 to 20 mil dry film, often in two coats — generally gives you many years of service, and it can be recoated later without a tear-off. Your written quote spells out the system, the number of coats and the manufacturer's warranty terms.

Q-04

Will a rubber coating stop an active leak?

+

Usually yes, when the deck underneath is still sound. The coating bridges pinholes, seams, fastener heads and hairline cracks and seals them under one continuous membrane. If we find wet insulation or rotted decking during the free inspection, that gets repaired first — coating over rot just hides the problem, and we won't do it.

Q-05

Does a rubber roof coating lower cooling bills?

+

A bright white or reflective elastic coating reflects a large share of the sun's energy instead of absorbing it, so the roof surface runs cooler and less heat pushes into the building. In a Central Louisiana summer that usually shows up as a more comfortable building and less strain on the AC. Actual savings depend on your insulation, ductwork and equipment.

Q-06

Is it cheaper than replacing the roof?

+

In most cases, yes — there's no tear-off, no dumpster and far less labor, so a coating typically costs a fraction of a full replacement. But it only makes sense if the structure and decking are sound. We inspect for free and give you an honest answer either way: coat it, repair it, or replace it.

Q-07

Do you coat roofs outside Alexandria?

+

Yes. We coat roofs across Central Louisiana — Alexandria, Pineville, Ball, Tioga, Boyce, Woodworth and the surrounding parishes. Give us a call and we'll tell you if you're in range.
